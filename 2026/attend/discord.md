The official IEEE ISMAR 2026 Discord server provides an additional space for communication and interaction among conference attendees before and during the event.

Use the server to receive conference updates, access dedicated discussion channels, and connect with other members of the ISMAR community.

## How to Join

Join the official ISMAR 2026 Discord server:
[Join the official ISMAR 2026 Discord server](https://discord.gg/7rAKWEWcc)

Locate the 16-digit registration token sent to the email address used for your ISMAR 2026 registration.

Open the #registration channel and use the /register command.

Enter your registration token and submit the command. Once the token is verified, the Registration Bot will automatically grant access to the conference channels.

## Important

Registration tokens are unique and can only be used once. Please do not share your token.

Use the /register command only in the #registration channel.

Please set a recognizable display name so that other attendees can identify you.

Discord participation is optional, but recommended as an additional communication channel alongside the official ISMAR 2026 website and conference announcements.

If you experience any issues accessing the server or verifying your registration, please post your request in the #help-desk channel.
